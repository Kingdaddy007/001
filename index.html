THE ARCHITECTURE OF AIR.

THRESHOLD is a tropical modernist design atelier. We build breathable residential sanctuaries for clients who seek absolute material honesty, fluid indoor-outdoor boundaries, and refuge from the equatorial sun.

We reject mid-market styling and artificial enclosures. We operate exclusively with native elements: slatted teak, warm laterite stone, expansive glass, and filtered cross-breezes.

THE PORTICO

Deep wooden overhangs casting precise, slatted shadows across sun-warmed laterite stone.

THE BATH

A limestone basin carved into a subterranean vault of rising steam.

THE WARDROBE

A sequence of illuminated glass and dark oak framing the ritual of dress.

THE TRANSIT

A polished corridor where shadow and light frame geometric studies of depth.

THE SALON

A sunken conversation space defined by sculptural seating and the quiet orchestration of natural light.

THE RESERVE

A climate-controlled sanctuary of illuminated glass and honed marble, protecting a curated vintage collection.
