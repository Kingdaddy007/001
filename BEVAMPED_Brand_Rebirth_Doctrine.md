### Pricing Posture Rules:

**State the price. Stop.** Never justify. Never explain hours worked. Explanation is submission.

**Never lower the price.** Reduce scope instead. The margin is sacred.

This builds more loyalty than any sales pitch. It signals that we protect their wallet. They will return and refer.

## 8. THE MOTION IDENTITY — How We Move

Motion is not decoration. Motion is a narrative tool.

**Principle:** Every animation must feel like light rising or ink setting — deliberate, weighted, refined.

### Non-Negotiable Motion Rules:

**Smooth scroll** via Lenis (duration: 1.5s, heavy initial resistance, feather deceleration)

**Scroll-triggered text reveals:** Characters rise from `y: 120%`, staggered at 20ms, `power4.out`

**No bouncing. No shaking.** Only: vertical reveals, horizontal translations, radial fades

**Fast deceleration:** 80% of animation completes in first 400ms — feels fast, but refined

**Gold ribbon:** Diagonal continuous translate on scroll — the brand's signature motion

### Motion Stack:

**GSAP** → all animations

**Lenis** → smooth scroll

**ScrollTrigger** → scroll-bound reveals

**Clip-path GSAP Draggable** → Before/After showroom reveals
