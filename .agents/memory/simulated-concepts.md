# Simulated Client Concepts for BEVAMPED

This document stores the three luxury interior designer client concepts we will simulate. We will build an incredible, motion-first digital showroom hero section for each of them.

---

## Concept A: "T H R E S H O L D" by Adebayo
* **Location:** Lagos / Milan
* **Studio Type:** Monolithic Brutalism & Raw Minimalism (concrete, obsidian, shadows).
* **Target Audience:** High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWIs) in West Africa & Southern Europe.
* **The Perception Gap:** They design ₦500M residential spaces, but their current site looks like a basic grid portfolio for a mid-market kitchen renovator.
* **Visual Thesis:** "The Architecture of Silence."
* **Hero Section Composition:** 
  * A dark viewport where a massive, raw concrete slab acts as a physical barrier.
  * As the cursor moves, a dynamic spotlight reveals the coarse grain of the concrete.
  * The title is layered behind the slab in 3D parallax space.
* **Motion Personality:** Glacial & Mechanical (slow, heavy, structural, high scroll-resistance).

---

## Concept B: "M A T I È R E" Studio
* **Location:** Dubai / Paris
* **Studio Type:** Soft Wabi-Sabi & Tactile Luxury (travertine, plaster, raw linen, open-grain oak).
* **Target Audience:** HNWIs desiring serene, organic residential sanctuaries.
* **The Perception Gap:** Their physical spaces are highly tactile and sensory, but their site is flat, sterile, and cold.
* **Visual Thesis:** "Form Follows Texture."
* **Hero Section Composition:**
  * Full-screen cinematic video loop of textured surfaces under moving sunlight.
  * On scroll, the video scales down and clips into a soft organic frame (curtain reveal).
  * Smooth, editorial text fades in below.
* **Motion Personality:** Liquid & Surgical (organic, smooth, seamless transitions).

---

## Concept C: "THE COLLECTOR'S ATELIER"
* **Location:** London / Lagos
* **Studio Type:** Theatrical Curation & Historical Eclecticism (rare art, marble, brass, velvet).
* **Target Audience:** Design-forward collectors, art enthusiasts, and luxury residential clients.
* **The Perception Gap:** They design bespoke, art-gallery-style homes, but their site looks like a generic retail catalog.
* **Visual Thesis:** "Living in Art."
* **Hero Section Composition:**
  * Asymmetric, overlapping grid displaying 3 distinct, high-definition objects (marble chair, brass light, velvet wall).
  * On scroll, the elements slide at staggered speeds (multi-plane parallax) to reveal the main brand message.
* **Motion Personality:** Brutal & Stuttered (bold, theatrical, abrupt scaling and positioning).
