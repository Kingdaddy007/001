# RESEARCH BRIEF: THRESHOLD by Adebayo

## Brand Summary

**Who:** THRESHOLD by Adebayo is an ultra-exclusive spatial design and brutalist residential architectural studio based in Lagos and Milan.
**What:** Designing massive, light-filled residential monoliths sculpted out of raw concrete, obsidian stone, and shadows.
**Why:** To create fortresses of silence and structural art that shield high-net-worth owners from the chaos of the outside world.
**How:** Rejecting the mid-market interior design cliché of wallpaper, trim, and soft styling. Focusing entirely on material integrity, structural mass, and natural light choreography.
**Personality:** Quietly Commanding, Monolithic, Restrained.
**Anti-references:** Generic template-based portfolios, soft pastel interior designers, mass-market residential developers.

## Audience Summary

**Who:** HNWIs in West Africa (Ikoyi, Victoria Island) and Southern Europe (Milan, Como). Primarily self-made tech founders, art collectors, real estate moguls, and industrial leaders who appreciate raw minimalism and high-end design.
**Needs:** A signature, structurally-sound residential build that stands as a piece of architectural art. They need a studio that understands heavy spatial engineering and can lead the project from concept to physical reality without compromise.
**Wants:** A silent, fortress-like sanctuary. They want their digital and physical environment to feel premium, museum-like, and highly intellectual.
**Objections:** Fearing the studio is just another "interior decorator" charging premium architectural fees without the actual structural and spatial mastery. Fearing the digital representation doesn't match the massive real-world scale of the projects.
**Language:** "Spatial volume," "material integrity," "light play," "monolithic form," "shadow lines."
**Proof points:** High-resolution interactive details showing raw stone grain, daylight transitions, and structural narratives of how complex physical constraints were solved.

## Competition Summary

**Main competitors:** Boutique interior design firms and standard residential architects in Lagos and Milan.
**What they're doing:** Displaying generic grid galleries of finished spaces on plain white backgrounds, listing generic service packages, and using soft, submissive brand postures.
**Synthesized Gap:** Competitors treat their digital portals as passive brochures. They fail to convey the physical scale, depth, and volume of the spaces. They look like commodity vendors, not architectural authorities.
**Our Opportunity:** Position THRESHOLD as an uncompromising practitioner of spatial silence. Create a digital showroom that feels physically heavy, where scrolling simulates walking through concrete thresholds, using interactive lighting to show material depth.

## Existing Materials

**Current website:** A generic Squarespace-like template with a white grid. Looks flat, lightweight, and fails to convey the weight and scale of concrete design.
**Brand assets:** Raw concrete texture libraries, high-contrast imagery, dark obsidian color preferences.
**Messaging:** "We design beautiful spaces." (Completely generic, needs replacement).

## Key Insights

**What's surprising:** Premium clients are not paying for "furniture selection"; they are paying for the *absence* of noise. They are buying spatial volume and light control.
**What's the opportunity:** The digital storefront must physically embody this. High margins and low volume mean we can afford an "out-of-the-box" immersive GSAP layout that repels low-value clients and excites design-literate HNWIs.
**What's the risk:** If the site feels too cold or brutalist without light, it can feel unlivable. We must balance raw concrete with warm light interactions.
**What's the one thing:** "We build silence. Shelter, not decoration."
