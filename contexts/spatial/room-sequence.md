# ROOM SEQUENCE: THRESHOLD by Adebayo

This sequence maps the visitor's digital journey through space and narrative stages:
`Atmosphere -> Taste -> Transformation -> Proof -> Method -> Inquiry`

---

## 1. Atmosphere (The Facade)
* **Room/Space Metaphor:** The Raw Concrete Gate.
* **Visitor Emotion:** Awe, anticipation, focus.
* **Primary Visual:** A massive, board-formed concrete monolith cutting through absolute dark shadows.
* **Copy Posture:** Silent, commanding. `FOR THOSE WHO SEEK SHELTER, NOT DECORATION.`
* **Proof/Decision Shown:** Uncompromising material select.
* **UI Responsibility:** Pinned full-viewport container, mouse-following light spill, glacial vertical entry animation.

## 2. Taste (The Gallery)
* **Room/Space Metaphor:** The Light-Shedding Void.
* **Visitor Emotion:** Serenity, relief, desire.
* **Primary Visual:** High-definition video loop of shifting daylight revealing raw concrete living spaces in Milan.
* **Copy Posture:** Visually literate, peer-to-peer. `THE CURATION OF SILENCE.`
* **Proof/Decision Shown:** Mastery over volume and natural light penetration.
* **UI Responsibility:** Scroll-bound curtain reveal wipe (monolith scales and translates left to mask-reveal the video).

## 3. Transformation (The Atelier)
* **Room/Space Metaphor:** The Casting Mold.
* **Visitor Emotion:** Intellectual curiosity, trust.
* **Primary Visual:** Dynamic morphing visual showing architectural blueprint lines shifting into physical, textured concrete walls.
* **Copy Posture:** Technical, authoritative. `FROM STRUCTURAL LOAD TO SILENT VOLUME.`
* **Proof/Decision Shown:** Solving severe spatial constraints (e.g., maximizing volume on a narrow Lagos land plot).
* **UI Responsibility:** Line-art sketch overlay blending into photorealistic video scrub on scroll.

## 4. Proof (The Monument)
* **Room/Space Metaphor:** The Lived Monolith.
* **Visitor Emotion:** Reassurance, conviction.
* **Primary Visual:** Zoomed-in texture study showing the fine joints and custom shadow-gaps of a finished monolith house.
* **Copy Posture:** Understated, post-completion truth.
* **Proof/Decision Shown:** HNWI client referral testimony rendered as an engraved textual inscription in concrete.
* **UI Responsibility:** Asymmetric, breathing grid (70% empty space), minimal typography, zero visual clutter.

## 5. Method (The Blueprint)
* **Room/Space Metaphor:** The Master Plan.
* **Visitor Emotion:** Professional safety, respect.
* **Primary Visual:** Stylized, high-contrast structural blueprints showing the VAMPED physical execution sequence.
* **Copy Posture:** Non-negotiable expert posture.
* **Proof/Decision Shown:** Clear pricing floor, engagement minimums, and paid diagnostic gate.
* **UI Responsibility:** Clean bento grid structure, high-contrast text, Montserrat caps metadata.

## 6. Inquiry (The Inner Sanctum)
* **Room/Space Metaphor:** The Threshold Door.
* **Visitor Emotion:** Intention, commitment, qualification.
* **Primary Visual:** A dark viewport with a single, glowing amber door frame outline at the center.
* **Copy Posture:** Highly selective, firm. `WE ACCEPT THREE COMMISSIONERS PER YEAR.`
* **Proof/Decision Shown:** Lead-qualification questionnaire (filtering budget and alignment).
* **UI Responsibility:** Interactive form fields, absolute black background, transition animation on focus.
