# BRAND-TO-SCENE TRANSLATION: THRESHOLD by Adebayo

## The Perception Gap
* **The Physical Caliber:** Adebayo designs and engineers ₦500M+ brutalist concrete residences with extreme spatial scale, custom slate joints, and sophisticated light control.
* **The Digital Caliber:** His current digital presence is a standard white-background grid website. It looks like a cheap decorator's template.
* **The Consequence:** HNWIs treat him as a commodity contractor, negotiating his design fees and requesting generic ornamental styles.
* **The Remedy:** We must build a digital showroom that feels physically heavy, editorial, and commanding, proving his design authority before the first call.

---

## Desired Visitor Belief
"Adebayo is not a decorator. He is an artist of spatial mass and light. His spaces are silent fortresses, and hiring him is a commission of structural art."

---

## The Taste World
* **Materials:** Coarse board-formed concrete, dark obsidian slate, brushed oil-rubbed bronze, cast iron.
* **Lighting:** High-contrast chiaroscuro, narrow shafts of low-angle amber sunlight cutting through dark spaces, deep shadows.
* **Geometry:** Monolithic blocks, vertical columns, massive voids, cantilevered planes.
* **Palette:** 70% Obsidian/Charcoal, 20% Bone Gray, 10% Gold/Amber.

---

## Enemy/Cliché to Reject
- The soft, pastel-colored interior decorator templates.
- Generic headlines ("Welcome to our portfolio").
- Fluffy adjectives ("beautiful, elegant, clean").
- Floating glassmorphism navbar menus.
- Standard 3-column photo grids.

---

## Spatial Metaphor
**"Passing through raw mass."**
The website is structured as a sequence of heavy physical thresholds. The visitor must "scroll through" heavy slabs, and move their cursor to let light "reveal" the space.

---

## Scene Sequence & Mapping

1. **Scene 1: The Monolith Facade (Hero Section)**
   * *Strategic Role:* Hook the visitor and filters out low-value inquiries.
   * *Composition:* A vertical board-formed concrete column slicing the dark screen. Massive serif text partially hidden behind it.
   * *Motion:* Monolith rises slowly (glacial). Staggered text reveal from behind the slab. Mouse-follow spotlight reveals the concrete's texture.
2. **Scene 2: The Living Monolith (Taste Gallery)**
   * *Strategic Role:* Demonstrate spatial volume and control of light.
   * *Composition:* Full-viewport video frame of sunlight shifting across a raw concrete living space in Milan.
   * *Motion:* Scroll-triggered wipe (curtain reveal). Monolith scales up to fill the viewport and slides left, revealing the living room.
3. **Scene 3: The Casting Mold (Atelier process)**
   * *Strategic Role:* Prove engineering depth and solve constraints.
   * *Composition:* Morphing overlay of sketch blueprints onto finished concrete columns.
   * *Motion:* Line sketch lines fade out as photo details slide in on scroll-scrub.
4. **Scene 4: The Monumental Inscription (Proof)**
   * *Strategic Role:* Establish trust and prestige.
   * *Composition:* HNWI client referral testimonial styled as an engraved textual inscription in concrete.
   * *Motion:* Quiet fade-in. Large areas of empty black space (70% breathing room).
5. **Scene 5: The Master Blueprint (Method)**
   * *Strategic Role:* Pre-qualify budget and outline the engagement floor.
   * *Composition:* Blueprint style bento grid outlining the VAMPED diagnostic audit.
   * *Motion:* Bento grid elements fade in with a slow stagger.
6. **Scene 6: The Amber Door (Inquiry Gate)**
   * *Strategic Role:* Capture highly-qualified leads.
   * *Composition:* A single amber light outline of a door frame in absolute darkness.
   * *Motion:* Text fields glow with warm amber borders on hover/focus.
