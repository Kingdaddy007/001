## PRODUCT SNAPSHOT

### Project Name

THRESHOLD by Adebayo

### One-Sentence Product Statement

A cinematic digital showroom that translates Adebayo's brutalist architectural spaces into an immersive, light-and-shadow digital experience that pre-qualifies HNWI clients before their first conversation.

### What This Product Is

An ultra-premium, interactive portfolio showroom focused on the hero section and entrance sequence. It demonstrates Adebayo's mastery over concrete, stone, shadows, and light through high-contrast typography, glacial motion, and immersive cursor-interactive lighting masks.

### What This Product Is Not

- A generic template-based photo grid (Squarespace, Wix, etc.)
- A standard mid-market interior decorator portfolio
- A lightweight, fast-loading, low-fidelity landing page

### Current Stage

Pre-launch / Simulated Concept Phase

### Definition Of Success

An out-of-the-box spatial experience that feels like walking through a physical, heavy concrete threshold. It must immediately communicate designer authority, enforce the "shelter, not decoration" copy stance, and feature a seamless GSAP/ScrollTrigger entry flow.

## USERS AND JOBS

### Primary Users

- **High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWIs - Lagos/Milan)** - Desiring silent, private, raw concrete residential fortress sanctuaries. High aesthetic standard, low tolerance for generic design, values rarity and craftsmanship.

### Secondary Users And Stakeholders

- **BEVAMPED Studio Team** - Evaluating the system's ability to compile written brand doctrines into high-end cinematic products.
- **Adebayo (Simulated Founder)** - Desiring a digital presence that matches the physical caliber of his ₦500M+ concrete structures.

### Core Jobs-To-Be-Done

1. When an HNWI arrives at the site, they want to immediately experience "design intelligence" through cinematic layout and motion, so they recognize Adebayo as an authority and accept premium rates without negotiation.
2. When a visitor scrolls, they want to witness light shifting across raw material, so they experience the spatial atmosphere of a concrete monolith digitally.

## CURRENT BUSINESS REALITY

### Active Priorities

1. Formulate the spatial storytelling assets and design thesis.
2. Define the visual system tokens (fonts, grid alignment, material behavior).
3. Implement the native TypeScript, GSAP, and Lenis smooth-scroll chassis.

### Important Constraints

- Focus on the **hero section entrance and first transition** (not a full multi-page site).
- Maintain strict compliance with the **BEVAMPED visual rules** (margins, breathing room, type hygiene) adapted for Adebayo's brand colors.

## SCOPE AND TRADEOFF POSTURE

### In Scope Right Now

- Spatial story mapping and visual thesis context files.
- Design tokens (`DESIGN.md` / `DESIGN.json`) for THRESHOLD.
- A fully built, cinematic hero section featuring a glacial vertical monolith entry, cursor spotlight reveals of concrete textures, and a ScrollTrigger curtain reveal transition.

### Explicitly Deferred

- Fully fledged multi-project case study pages.
- Contact form backend (we will only design the inquiry gate layout).

### Preferred Tradeoffs

- Prefer premium visual caliber and weighted motion over lightweight simplicity.
- Avoid generic web layouts (like centered navigation or card-based grids) in favor of editorial, museum-like layouts.
